Official Press Release — December 2024

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

From the Darkness of Grief to the Light of Healing: Virginia R. Jacobs Releases Emotional New Single "I Wanna Feel"

December 2024 - Virginia R. Jacobs, an artist renowned for her emotional depth and powerful vocal presence, releases her latest single, "I Wanna Feel." The song offers a raw and intimate reflection of the artist's journey through grief, depression, and ultimately, healing.

"I Wanna Feel" is now available for streaming on all major platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube.

Virginia R. Jacobs is a gifted singer-songwriter whose music bridges emotional vulnerability and artistic expression.
