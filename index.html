Official Press Release — December 2024

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

From the Darkness of Grief to the Light of Healing: Virginia R. Jacobs Releases Emotional New Single "I Wanna Feel"

December 2024 - Virginia R. Jacobs, an artist renowned for her emotional depth and powerful vocal presence, releases her latest single, "I Wanna Feel." The song offers a raw and intimate reflection of the artist's journey through grief, depression, and ultimately, healing. With this poignant new track, Virginia invites listeners into her world of vulnerability and resilience, sharing a deeply personal story that touches on universal themes of emotional healing and the power of music.

"I Wanna Feel" is not just another song; it is an emotional roadmap. Written three years ago during one of the darkest periods of Virginia's life, the track was born out of her pain following the loss of two people who meant the world to her. The subsequent emotional turmoil and battle with severe depression led Virginia to search for solace through music and journaling. These therapeutic outlets became the foundation for the heartfelt lyrics that form the heart of this new single.

"This song is my emotional release, a way to process the grief I felt after losing two important people in my life," Said Virginia.

The track features a blend of easy-listening and soothing melodies, making it the perfect backdrop for listeners seeking reflection and emotional release. Virginia's powerful voice carries the weight of her experiences, while the lyrics speak directly to those who have ever yearned for healing from their own pain.

"I Wanna Feel" appeals to listeners of all ages, with themes that are particularly resonant for anyone navigating loss, longing, or emotional recovery. While the song's primary audience includes those aged 18 and up, the message is universal. Whether someone is reflecting on the end of a relationship or coping with the death of a loved one, the song's message of transformation is meant to resonate with anyone who has ever experienced the intense pull between grief and the hope for healing.

The heart of the song lies within its chorus, which captures the pain and longing that Virginia felt during her struggles. The chorus questions the listener's own emotional journey, prompting reflection on personal experiences of loss and recovery. "The lyrics are based on real things I've said to people. When I wrote the music, I wanted to create a piece that makes the listener feel what I felt," Virginia explained. "The chorus is the most powerful part of the song-it invites people to connect with their own emotions, their own struggles, and their own desire for healing."

In addition to the heartfelt lyrics, Virginia's artistry shines through in her ability to craft melodies that are both engaging and emotionally evocative. "As an artist, I believe part of my job is to make people feel something-to take them on a journey through sound and lyrics," she said. "I hope 'I Wanna Feel' touches your heart as deeply as it touched mine. This song is an invitation to heal, to reflect, and to embrace the journey of emotional recovery."

The release of "I Wanna Feel" marks a pivotal moment in Virginia R. Jacobs' career as she steps into a new chapter as a solo artist. Her raw, vulnerable approach to songwriting and her ability to convey deep emotional truths through music has already garnered attention from fans and critics alike.

"I Wanna Feel" is now available for streaming on all major platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube

About Virginia R. Jacobs

Virginia R. Jacobs is a gifted singer-songwriter whose music bridges emotional vulnerability and artistic expression. Known for her ability to connect deeply with her audience, her music speaks to themes of personal transformation, healing, and love. Virginia's work is shaped by her life experiences, creating music that resonates with listeners on a deeply personal level.
