    Official Press Release

    For Immediate Release

    Virginia R. Jacobs releases I Wanna Feel, an original song about love, courage, and feeling visible.

    Virginia R. Jacobs is a blind singer-songwriter, pianist, and motivational voice whose music blends honest storytelling with soulful pop.

    About the Single: I Wanna Feel explores the universal desire to be seen, heard, and emotionally understood.

    “I want people to feel something real when they hear my music.” — Virginia R. Jacobs

    Listen to I Wanna Feel and follow Virginia’s official channels for new music and releases.
